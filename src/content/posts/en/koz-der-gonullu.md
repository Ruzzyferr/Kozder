At KOZ-DER we want to build stronger projects with the ideas, energy and commitment of young people.

We are looking for volunteers who want to contribute to change, to create, to work together on stronger initiatives and to gain experience in civil society. If you would like to contribute in youth work, project development, social media, design, technology, event organisation or communication, you can be part of the KOZ-DER family.

## Where We Are Looking for Volunteers

We would like to work with young people who want to volunteer in the following areas:

- **Social Media**
  Content creation, planning, publishing and social media design

- **Project Writing**
  Project development, drafting applications, writing and reporting

- **Project Management**
  Planning, delivery, coordination and progress tracking

- **Event Organisation**
  Generating event ideas, preparation, delivery and participant processes

- **Partners and Communication**
  Communicating with organisations, developing cooperation and building networks

- **Design and Visual Communication**
  Graphic design, posters, presentations, announcements and visual content

- **Technology and Innovation**
  Web and digital solutions, software, app ideas and technical support

## Who Can Apply?

- Young people who want to volunteer
- People who work well in a team
- Anyone who wants experience in civil society
- People who enjoy generating ideas, taking responsibility and working together
- Anyone with interest or experience in social media, project writing, design, technology or events
- Volunteers who want to make a concrete contribution to KOZ-DER's work

## Why Volunteer with KOZ-DER?

As a KOZ-DER volunteer you do not simply support a piece of work: you build new skills, get to know project processes from the inside, gain teamwork experience and take an active role in the youth field.

We think together, create together and build the future together.

## How to Apply

Young people who would like to volunteer can visit our website for details and to apply.
