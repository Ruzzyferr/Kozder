Applications for the ESC 2026 period are now open.

Candidates should complete their applications within the stated period. Applications are made through the Turna Portal.

Application links and project calls are available at kozder.com. Announcements about the process, application details and guidance are also published on our website, so please check it regularly while applications are open.

## Deadline

- Deadlines differ from project to project — please check the relevant project.

## Who Can Apply?

- Young people aged 18-30
- Candidates willing to take part in community projects
- Participants who work well in a team
- Candidates with knowledge or experience in intelligence games and robotics and coding
- Candidates who want to make a concrete contribution to the association
