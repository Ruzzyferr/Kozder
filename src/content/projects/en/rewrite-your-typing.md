## ⚡ Urgent call for participants

We are looking for **4 young people aged 18–30** and **1 group leader** for the **(Re)write Your Typing** Erasmus+ youth exchange in Poland.

**Green passport required:** The project starts very soon, so there is no time for a visa process. We are therefore looking for applicants with a **Turkish green (special) passport**, who can travel to the Schengen area without a visa.

**Application deadline: 10 October 2026**

## About the project

Every day we type, share and produce countless words. This youth exchange is built on one idea: instead of treating digital tools as a threat to authentic communication, we can use them as **creative supports that make our voices heard**. The goal is to break the cycle of mindless typing and build genuine connections.

Participants move together from automatic, reactive texting to **intentional, reflective self-expression**, combining:

- **Writing:** the structural foundation of expression
- **Photography:** visual narratives that anchor written emotion
- **Drama:** voice, tone and empathy through physical performance
- **Artificial intelligence:** a collaborative sparring partner, not a replacement for human thought

The programme also includes an **intercultural evening** built on food, music, dance and games rather than slide presentations.

## Who can apply?

- Young people aged 18–30 (4 places) and 1 group leader with no age limit
- Anyone who feels misunderstood or simply wants to explore the creative side of their everyday writing
- No special writing skills needed; **English A2–B1** is enough
- **Holders of a Turkish green (special) passport**

## Accommodation and travel

- **Arrival:** 16 October 2026 · **Departure:** 23 October 2026
- **Venue:** Puck, Poland, a small town on the Baltic Sea coast
- Three meals and two coffee breaks a day; towels provided.
- Rooms shared with participants of the same gender and different nationality. No single rooms.
- **Nearest airport:** Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa (GDN). Alternatively Warsaw Chopin (WAW) or Modlin (WMI), then train or Flixbus to Gdańsk.
- Frequent trains run from Gdańsk to Puck.
- Travel costs are covered by the organiser; arrangements are agreed individually with selected participants and their sending organisation.

**Important:** Do not book tickets, buy insurance or make other preparations until you have official confirmation from the organiser.

## Insurance

Valid travel health insurance is mandatory: a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) or equivalent private insurance covering medical emergencies, accidents, lost belongings and travel disruption.
