## About the Project

As KOZ-DER we are looking for participants holding a green passport for a European Solidarity Corps (ESC) project. The project brings young people together with participants from other cultures in an international setting, gives them volunteering experience and involves them actively in intercultural learning.

This mobility is a valuable opportunity for anyone who wants to gain volunteering experience abroad at short notice, who is interested in ESC projects and who can contribute actively throughout the process.

## Participant Profile

Applicants are expected to hold a green passport. It is important that participants have no travel restrictions, can take an active part in the project and have the motivation to work well within an international team.

Applicants are expected to:

- Hold a green passport
- Be interested in ESC and volunteering projects
- Work well in a team
- Be open to intercultural learning
- Be willing to communicate with participants from other countries
- Take an active and responsible part throughout the project

## What Participation Involves

Participants take an active part in volunteering activities, workshops, group work, cultural exchange and social events throughout the project. This is not simply a trip abroad; it is a process of learning, sharing, solidarity, personal growth and getting to know other cultures.

## Expected Outcomes

- Participants gain international volunteering experience
- Intercultural communication skills develop
- Awareness of ESC projects increases
- Teamwork, self-confidence and active participation are strengthened
- Participants gain experience of new countries, cultures and volunteering work
