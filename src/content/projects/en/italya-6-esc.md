## About the Project

KOZ-DER Go to Italy 6 is a short-term ESC volunteering project taking place in Treviso, Italy between 15.06.2026 and 31.07.2026. It is an education-based placement working with children and young people, in which volunteers support teachers in the classroom as teaching assistants.

Volunteers run English activities, game-based learning, creative workshops and intercultural exchange, mainly with primary school children. The role is not that of a formal teacher: it is a supporting one, built around classroom help, interactive learning and letting children experience English in a more natural and enjoyable way.

## Core Activities

- Preparing and running English activities for primary school pupils
- Supporting teachers in the classroom as a teaching assistant
- Contributing to learning through games, stories, songs and creative materials
- Supporting pronunciation, vocabulary and basic speaking skills
- Helping prepare classroom visuals, worksheets and simple learning aids
- Running creative workshops, drama, storytelling and group work
- Organising activities where volunteers can share their own culture with the children
- Where appropriate, contributing to intelligence games, educational group work and robotics and coding activities

## Participant Profile

The project suits volunteers who want to work with children, can take an active role in an educational setting, communicate well and think creatively. A willingness to make English fun and understandable for children is a real advantage.

Participants are expected to be responsible, open to teamwork, able to work well in an intercultural environment and comfortable taking a supporting role in a school. Candidates with experience in non-formal education, running activities for children, intelligence games, robotics and coding, creative drama, group games or similar educational work are at a particular advantage.

## Accommodation and Meals

Volunteers stay in a shared flat in or near Treviso, provided by the organisation or local partners. The accommodation is new, well kept and equipped for everyday living, with a kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms, Wi-Fi and basic furniture. Living together is treated as an important part of the learning: it builds cultural exchange and everyday life skills.

Meals and daily living are supported under ESC rules.

## Travel Support

Travel is arranged in line with ESC rules. Please plan transport and travel support according to the organisation's guidance, and read the info pack carefully before applying.

## Pocket Money and Support

Volunteers receive the support provided for under ESC rules. Mentoring, organisational support, help settling in locally, training planning and day-to-day guidance are also provided. Participants receive a Youthpass certificate at the end of the project.

## Expected Outcomes

- Participants gain experience of working with children
- Skills in classroom support, teaching assistance and adapting to an educational setting develop
- English communication, teamwork and intercultural awareness are strengthened
- Participants gain experience in game-based learning, creative activities and non-formal education
- Volunteers grow in confidence, responsibility and communication
