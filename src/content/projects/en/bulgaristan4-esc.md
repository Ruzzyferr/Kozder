## About the Project

This ESC project is designed as a two-month volunteering placement for two volunteers. Throughout the project the volunteers take part in intelligence games, robotics and coding, environmental clean-ups and non-formal education activities. The project supports the skills development of children and young people, raises environmental awareness and strengthens social solidarity.

## Core Activities

- Running intelligence-game workshops
- Supporting robotics and coding activities
- Carrying out environmental clean-ups and awareness work
- Taking part in non-formal education and volunteering activities for the community
- Running local activities that encourage teamwork, solidarity and active participation

## Expected Outcomes

- Two volunteers take an active part in the project for two months
- Participants build skills in intelligence games and in robotics and coding
- Environmental awareness and a sense of social responsibility grow
- Interaction and intercultural learning between volunteers and the local community are strengthened
- Social solidarity is supported through volunteering
