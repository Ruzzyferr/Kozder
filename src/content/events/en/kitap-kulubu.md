The KOZ-DER Book Club continues with a new meeting. This month we come together to remember İlber Ortaylı, who passed away recently, and to think together about his book "Bir Ömür Nasıl Yaşanır?" (How Should a Life Be Lived?).

## What to Expect

- Reflections and comments on the book
- General conversation about the author and the work
- Group discussion where everyone can share their thoughts
- A social space that supports a culture of reading
- New book suggestions and ideas for future meetings

The Book Club is a good meeting point for anyone who wants to share what they have read, listen to different perspectives and enjoy good conversation.
