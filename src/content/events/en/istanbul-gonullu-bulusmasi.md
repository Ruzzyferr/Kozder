KOZ-DER is meeting up in Istanbul with our volunteers, former participants and young people interested in new projects. We invite everyone who would like to talk about the European Solidarity Corps (ESC), Erasmus+ opportunities, volunteering experience and international projects.

This meet-up is a good chance to get to know KOZ-DER better, hear from former volunteers, learn about new project opportunities and meet young people with similar goals.

## What to Expect

- An introduction to the KOZ-DER volunteer network
- An overview of ESC and Erasmus+ projects
- Experiences from volunteers who have taken part in projects
- Conversation about new application processes and opportunities
- Meeting other volunteers and building a network
- Questions and answers, and one-to-one chats

## Who Can Come?

Any young person interested in ESC, Erasmus+ and international youth projects — whether you want to apply to a KOZ-DER project, learn about volunteering or meet people who have taken part before.

We would also be glad to see volunteers who have joined a project through KOZ-DER come along and share their experience.

- Time: 19:00
- Venue: Espresso Lab Mecidiyeköy, Istanbul
