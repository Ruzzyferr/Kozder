We are taking the excitement of European Youth Week online. We are running a webinar to talk through your questions about youth opportunities, European projects and international experience.

At KOZ-DER we would like to invite all young people to this online meeting, where we will introduce opportunities supported by the European Union and the Turkish National Agency, share our experience and answer your questions.

The webinar takes place on **29 April 2026** at **21:00** on Google Meet.

## What to Expect

- An introduction to European Youth Week
- Youth opportunities and international project possibilities
- An overview of the Erasmus+ and ESC programmes
- Routes into European projects
- Experience sharing and a question-and-answer session

This webinar is a good opportunity for anyone who wants to broaden their horizons, discover new opportunities and learn more about European projects.
