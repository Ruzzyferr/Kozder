KOZ-DER is taking part in the **Civil Voices Festival** in Ankara on **5 June 2026**. At our stand we will share information about volunteering, youth opportunities and ESC and Erasmus+ projects, and meet our volunteers, young people and anyone who would like to get to know us.

We look forward to welcoming our volunteers in Ankara, young people who have taken part in our projects before, and everyone interested in international opportunities. Throughout the festival you can get to know KOZ-DER better and ask us directly about ESC, Erasmus+ and volunteering.

## What to Expect

- An introduction to KOZ-DER
- Information about volunteering and youth opportunities
- Details on ESC and Erasmus+ projects
- A chance to meet volunteers who have taken part in projects
- Conversation about new project opportunities
- Questions and answers, and one-to-one chats

## Who Can Come?

Anyone interested in volunteering, youth work, ESC, Erasmus+ and international project opportunities. We would especially like to meet young people in Ankara, our volunteers, and anyone who wants to get to know KOZ-DER.

## Event Details

- Date: 5 June 2026
- Time: 13:00 - 17:00
- Venue: Festival Area (Esat Hall), Ankara
