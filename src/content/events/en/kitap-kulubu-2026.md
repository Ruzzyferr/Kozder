# The KOZ-DER Book Club Is Starting! 📚

Are you ready to meet between the pages of a book, discover new ideas and grow through different perspectives?

With the Book Club launching under **KOZ-DER**, we are setting out to support a culture of reading, share our thoughts through books and create a space where we can learn together. 🤍

Because we believe books are not only for reading, but also for **thinking, questioning, making sense of things and coming a little closer to other people's worlds**.

Many lovely conversations await us, as we meet around a different book each month, share our ideas freely and make sense of what we read together.

## 📖 Our First Book: *Jonathan Livingston Seagull* – Richard Bach

Together, we are opening the first page of new beginnings, inspiring conversations and friendships that grow with books. 🌱

## Event Details

- **First meeting:** Monday, 30 November 2026
- **Time:** 21:00 (Türkiye time)
- **First book:** *Jonathan Livingston Seagull* – Richard Bach
- **Frequency:** A different book every month
- **Coordinator:** Elmira Dursun
