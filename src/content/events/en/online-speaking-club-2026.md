The long-awaited **Online Speaking Club** is starting! In these sessions prepared by KOZ-DER, we combine English speaking practice with **debates**, **discussions** and other interactive activities to help participants build their English communication skills.

The goal is not perfect English. It is to **speak, practise, explore new perspectives** and feel more comfortable using English in everyday life.

- Rana Karakulak

## Details

- Starts: Wednesday 14 October 2026
- Day: Every Wednesday (ongoing)
- Time: 21:00 (Türkiye time)
- Place: Online
- Participation: Completely free
- Places: Limited

## How to join

If you would like to join, first fill in the **level assessment form**. Your answers help us plan sessions and content that match participants' levels.

[Click here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScUinJuiO4lJMbda2OJYMwlwaOSCo7NT96jk3lGdCpQkS4wrQ/viewform) for the level assessment and application form.

You can also join our in-person [KOZ-DER Speaking Club](/en/events/speakingclub-2026) every Friday in Kilis.

**Speak, share, practise and improve your English!**
