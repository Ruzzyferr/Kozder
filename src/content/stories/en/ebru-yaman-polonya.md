# A Short Project, Memories to Remember for a Long Time

Sometimes a few days can add far more to our lives than we expect. The **(RE)WRITE YOUR TYPING** Erasmus+ youth exchange I joined in Puck, Poland, from 16 to 23 October was exactly that kind of experience for me. It was **my second Erasmus+ project**, and being part of it together with our coordinator, and once again leaving the project very satisfied, made it extra special.

## Writing, Thinking and Learning Together

During the project we took part in many workshops on **creative writing, positive thinking and creativity**. We wrote a lot in English, tried to express our thoughts in different ways and improved our writing skills. Our groups changed constantly, which sometimes made things a bit complicated, but it helped us understand each other better, discover different perspectives and realise how similarly we can think as people from different countries.

One of the most important things this six-day experience gave me was **learning to think like a writer.** One of my favourite activities was the creative session where we made postcards for ourselves. By putting together different papers, cutting and gluing, we created our own postcards and small journals. It was great fun and a chance to express ourselves freely.

## Telling Türkiye's Story: The Intercultural Evening

The intercultural evening was one of the most special moments of the project for me. We were a little nervous at first because we wanted to represent Türkiye in the best possible way. The five of us organised everything together, and I think we created a lovely presentation we were all happy with.

We started our presentation by serving our friends **Turkish coffee**. Then, beginning with our flag and Atatürk, we introduced our culture, traditional dishes and drinks, and historical sites. We continued the evening with a **henna night, wedding music and our folk dances**. It was truly wonderful to see our friends take an interest in our culture, try our treats and dance with us.

Getting to know the cultures of other countries was just as enjoyable as presenting our own. While learning about different traditions, we also saw how similar we really are. We may not have had much time to explore Puck, but over six days we all went on a small cultural journey together.

## Much More Than a Week

What remains from this project is not only beautiful memories but also **new friendships, different perspectives and new skills in expressing myself**. I was very happy with my fellow participants and the activities overall. Once again I understood how valuable it is to create together, share different ideas and find a common language with new people.

I would like to thank **KOZ-DER**, our host organisation **Fundacja Autokreacja**, our coordinator and all my friends who made this week special.

**For me, (RE)WRITE YOUR TYPING will remain not just a six-day project in Poland, but an experience in which I rediscovered writing, thinking, sharing and connecting with people.** 🤍
