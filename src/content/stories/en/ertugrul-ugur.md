Hello everyone,

I am **Ertuğrul Uğur**. On **13.04.2026** I joined an Erasmus+ project in Bulgaria. It was a valuable experience for me, both enjoyable and instructive.

I think the project went very well overall. Coming together with people from other cultures, listening to their perspectives and being in the same space for a shared purpose meant a great deal to me. Meeting new people every day, encountering different ideas and sharing them together gave me a lot.

## What I Experienced

Throughout the project I did not only take part in activities; spending time with participants from other countries taught me a great deal culturally too. Seeing how people live, how they think and how they express themselves helped me look at things from a wider angle.

I also think the project was well run in terms of the content and planning of the activities and the teamwork. Through group work and shared tasks I improved my communication and came to understand better the importance of creating together. Sometimes even a short project can leave a lasting mark on someone's life; this one did exactly that for me.

## What the Project Gave Me

I believe I grew both personally and socially. Adapting to a new environment, communicating with different people and acting together gave me confidence. I also saw once again how much being among people from other cultures enriches you.

Throughout the project I felt more open-minded, more adaptable and stronger. Seeing for myself that difference is not an obstacle but something that helps you grow meant a lot.

## Final Word

Looking at it as a whole, the project gave me a great deal both socially and personally. I did not only meet new people; I gained new perspectives. It will stay with me as an experience I will not forget.

Thank you to everyone who contributed to this wonderful experience.
