## First Time Abroad, First Erasmus+: New Friendships in Pazardzhik 🇧🇬

Hi, I’m Ahmet from Adana. I have graduated from high school and will soon start university.

Through **KOZ-DER**, I took part in the **Digital Democracy: Youth Voices Online!** Erasmus+ youth exchange held in **Pazardzhik**, Bulgaria, from 28 October to 6 November 2026. As it was both **my first trip abroad** and **my first Erasmus+ experience**, this project holds a special place for me.

## New Friendships from Different Countries

The best part of the project for me was meeting and talking with people from different countries. Learning about the places they live, their daily lives and their ideas was a real pleasure.

Being together with people from different cultures and **discovering what we have in common** as we talked is what stays with me most from this experience.

Thanks to the time we spent together, I made friends from different countries. Coming back with such friendships from my very first trip abroad means a lot to me.

## Learning Together

Throughout the project, we took part in activities on **digital democracy, media literacy, tackling misinformation and young people’s participation in the digital world**. Exchanging ideas on these topics with young people from different countries was a great chance to look at the same issue from different angles.

Group work also helped us get to know each other and learn together. Being part of an international environment where everyone could freely share their thoughts was a completely new experience for me.

## Improving My English by Using It

One of my most important gains was **improving my English**. I had the chance to use English all day to communicate and get to know people.

Chatting, trying to understand others and explaining my own ideas was valuable practice. In this project I experienced first-hand **how learning a language helps us build connections with people**.

## A Great Start

Having this experience before starting university will stay with me as a beautiful memory. I came back from Pazardzhik with new friendships and better English.

When I think of my first Erasmus+ experience, what I will remember most is the joy of coming together and spending time with people from different countries.

## Thank You

I thank **KOZ-DER** for this opportunity, our host organisation **Association FORUM**, and all the friends I shared this experience with. 💙
