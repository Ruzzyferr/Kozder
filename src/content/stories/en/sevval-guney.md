I am **Şevval Güney**. I took part in the **Truth or Trick Erasmus+ Training Course** held in **Madan**, Bulgaria between **13.07.2026 and 20.07.2026**.

It was my first Erasmus+ project, and I will never forget what I experienced and learned there.

## My First Erasmus+ Experience

As a psychology student I got a great deal out of this project. Throughout the course we gained new knowledge and turned it into activities, which made the learning far more enjoyable.

Although the project lasted only seven days it was completely full for me. Every day I learned something new, gained a different perspective and grew.

## Good Days in Madan

Madan is a small, peaceful town with wonderful views. In this beautiful place we came together with friends from other countries, shared our cultures, laughed, had fun and collected unforgettable memories.

Thanks to the friendships we made, Madan became a very special place for us.

## What It Meant to Me

The project went far better than I had imagined. Between what I learned and the friendships I built, it was an unforgettable Erasmus+ experience.

Thank you very much to **KOZ-DER** for giving me this opportunity.
