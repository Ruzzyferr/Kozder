## My Erasmus+ Experience in Bulgaria: Digital Democracy 🇧🇬

I recently returned from the **Digital Democracy: Youth Voices Online!** Erasmus+ youth exchange I joined in **Pazardzhik**, Bulgaria, from 28 October to 6 November 2026.

After these intense and productive days of discussing and creating together around **democracy, youth participation and active citizenship** in the digital age, I wanted to briefly share what stayed with me.

## New People, Through the Eyes of an English Literature Student

The most enjoyable part of the project was meeting, getting to know and socialising with people from different countries and cultures.

As a student of **English Language and Literature**, actively using English not only in classes or written texts but in the natural flow of daily life, **communicating with many different accents**, was very rewarding for me.

Listening directly to different perspectives and expressing my own ideas in this multicultural environment enriched both my command of the language and the way I see the topics we discussed.

## Amateur Photography 📷

Bulgaria’s unique character, its calm streets and the energetic moments we shared during the project gave me a great setting to practise **amateur photography**.

Exploring the city between activities, I experimented a lot with light, framing and catching the right moment. Photographing both the atmosphere of the places I visited and my friends’ most natural, candid moments made this experience even more meaningful and enjoyable. The city photos on this page come from those experiments.

## Old and New Erasmus+ Friends

While making new friends in this project, I stayed in constant touch with friends from my previous Erasmus+ projects. Sharing my observations, experiences and photos from Bulgaria with them created a lovely bridge between our past experiences and today.

Seeing this network, built over different times and different projects, keep growing is **one of the best things Erasmus+ has given me**.

## In Short

These were ten full days in which I met new people, practised photography and came home with beautiful memories. If you are thinking about joining an Erasmus+ project, **give it a chance without hesitation**.

Thank you to **KOZ-DER** for making this experience possible, to our host organisation **Association FORUM**, and to my fellow travellers. 💙
