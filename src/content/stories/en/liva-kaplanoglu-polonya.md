# My First Erasmus+ Experience: An Unforgettable Week in Puck 🇵🇱❤️

Through **KOZ-DER**, I took part in the **(RE)WRITE YOUR TYPING** project in Puck, Poland. It was **my first Erasmus+ experience**, and when I set off I really didn't know what was waiting for me. I was both excited and a little nervous. A new country, different cultures and people I had never met before… But I never imagined that by the end of the week I would have met so many wonderful people, or that leaving would make me so sad.

During the project we met people from different countries, took part in workshops together, talked and laughed a lot. In the first days we were trying to get to know each other, and a few days later it felt as if we had been friends for much longer.

I think this is the best thing about these projects. You come together with people who grew up in different countries and lived different lives. At first you don't know much about each other, but over time **you discover what you have in common.** Sometimes a conversation, sometimes a small joke, sometimes even an ordinary evening together becomes the best moment of the day.

As we spent time together, I didn't only learn about other countries; I also got to know the lives, thoughts and habits of the people who live there. Everyone had a different story to tell, and listening to them was truly lovely.

## The Intercultural Evenings Were My Favourite!

My favourite part of the week was definitely the intercultural evenings!

Serving **Turkish coffee**, sharing our **Turkish delight** and holding a **henna night** to introduce Türkiye was wonderful. Talking about our own culture, explaining our traditions and seeing our friends listen with curiosity made me very happy.

The henna night was especially fun for me. Sharing our own traditions with people from other countries, and having them join in, was an amazing feeling. While telling them about our culture, we also had the chance to learn about theirs.

We also had a lot of fun at the other countries' intercultural evenings. We tried different foods, listened to music and danced. Everyone bringing something from their own country, discovering traditions very different from ours and having fun together are among the best memories of the week.

## The Hardest Part Was Saying Goodbye

I have to admit that before the project I didn't think I could form such strong bonds in just one week. After all, we all came from different countries and would only be together for a few days.

But as the days went by, the idea of saying goodbye became harder and harder. The people I ate with, talked with, laughed with and spent the evenings with were no longer just people I had met in a project.

When the last day came, I wished we had a little more time. Wanting to spend more time with everyone, to talk a little longer and to not let those days end so soon felt completely natural.

Thank you to all the friends I met during the project. I am so happy to have met each of you. I hope we stay in touch, hear from each other and meet again one day. Maybe in another Erasmus project, or maybe on a day when we visit each other's countries…

I would also like to thank our group leader Ebru for her support and for everything. And many thanks to **KOZ-DER** for their care. ❤️

## I'm So Glad I Went!

When I left for Puck, I had many questions in my mind. On the way back, I had beautiful memories and people I want to see again.

I didn't know I would leave my first Erasmus+ experience this happy. Seeing a new country was lovely, but for me the best part was **the people I met there.** The things we experienced together during that week, our conversations and the laughter we shared are very precious to me.

Now, when I think about those days, a smile appears on my face. Looking at the photos takes me back to those moments, and I wish it had lasted a little longer.

I left Puck, but I don't think I will easily forget the people I met there and the days we spent together.

**That was my first Erasmus+ experience. I'm so glad I went, and so glad I met all of you. I hope this isn't goodbye, just a short break until the day we meet again.** ❤️
