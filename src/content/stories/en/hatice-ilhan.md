I am **Hatice İlhan**. Between **April and June 2025** I took part in a short-term European Solidarity Corps project in Bulgaria.

The experience holds a very important place in my life, both personally and professionally. Working alongside people from other cultures gave me the chance to grow and to widen how I see the world.

## My Volunteering

Throughout the placement I was active in the field. I worked directly with local people on environment-focused projects and contributed to work aimed at building social awareness. Coming into closer contact with nature, with people and with different ways of living gave me a great deal.

The project did not limit me to Bulgaria: I also had the chance to visit **Spain, Italy and France** and experience other cultures first-hand. Those journeys helped me develop a more open view of the world, and every new experience changed me a little.

## What This Gave Me

The experience did not only teach me to take responsibility. It also showed me how to build empathy, understand difference and appreciate the power of creating together. It was a journey in which I learned something new every day and felt freer and stronger.

Working with people from other countries improved my communication and increased my confidence. I noticed how deep a mark even a very small interaction can leave on a person.

## Looking Back

Looking back today, the most important things this experience gave me were the bonds I built with people and the awareness I gained. Volunteering is not only about contributing to society; it is a valuable journey in which you discover yourself, redefine your limits and learn to see life differently.

I feel lucky to have been part of this and to have crossed paths with **KOZ-DER**. This experience will always hold a special place in my life.
