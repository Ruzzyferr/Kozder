## A Volunteering Story in the Rhodope Mountains: 56 Days in Madan

Hello everyone,

I am **Sefa Ocak**. Sometimes a project gives you far more than you expected. When I set off for **Madan**, one of Bulgaria's small but warm towns, I did not really know what lay ahead. All I knew was that I would be volunteering on the **ARTEVS** project between **12 April and 7 June 2026**. But the journey turned into much more than a volunteering project: it became a very special part of my life, full of new friendships, unforgettable memories and personal growth.
