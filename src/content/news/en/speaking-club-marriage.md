# This Week at KOZ-DER Speaking Club: Marriage

🗣️💬 **KOZ-DER Speaking Club continues at full speed!**

Every week, we come together around a different discussion topic. This week's theme was **“Marriage.”** 💍✨

Our participants discussed the concept of marriage, different cultural perspectives on marriage, expectations, relationships, family structures and individual opinions — all in English.

While sharing their ideas, participants had the opportunity to practise speaking, expand their vocabulary and express themselves more confidently.

This is exactly what makes Speaking Club special: it is not only about speaking English, but also about **listening to different perspectives, learning new words, expressing yourself more comfortably and improving together.** 🌍🤝

In a friendly and interactive environment, participants actively used English in everyday communication while also listening to different opinions and exchanging perspectives.

With KOZ-DER Speaking Club, we continue to meet every week for **a new topic, a new conversation and a new experience.**

📅 **See you every Friday at 20:00 at KOZ-DER Speaking Club!**
