## KOZ-DER Was Represented at Bridges for Futures-2 in Bremen, Germany

As the Youth Intelligence Games and Coding Association **(KOZ-DER)**, we continue to strengthen our international youth work and our cooperation at European level.

Our Vice President **Halil ÖZKARACA** represented the association at the **Bridges for Futures-2** project, hosted by **NaturKultur e.V.** in **Bremen**, Germany between **2 and 8 August 2026**.

## New Partnerships and International Cooperation

Throughout the project, meetings were held with youth workers, civil society representatives and organisation representatives from a range of countries, covering good practice in the youth field, international project experience and possible joint work in the future.

Contacts were established with new organisations on behalf of KOZ-DER, and new partnership opportunities were explored within the framework of Erasmus+ and the European Solidarity Corps **(ESC)**.

## A Network Growing from the Local to Europe

As a Kilis-based youth association, KOZ-DER continues to work towards increasing young people's access to international opportunities, strengthening their engagement with other cultures and building sustainable partnerships with youth organisations across Europe.

**Bridges for Futures-2** was an important opportunity for KOZ-DER to raise its visibility in Europe, meet new partners and widen its partnership network in the youth field.

## New Opportunities for Young People

Thanks to these new connections, we aim to give young people access to more international opportunities in the period ahead through Erasmus+, ESC, training courses, youth exchanges and volunteering projects.

KOZ-DER will keep developing new projects that contribute to young people's growth, support intercultural learning and strengthen international solidarity.

## Thank You

We would like to thank the **NaturKultur e.V.** team for hosting **Bridges for Futures-2**, all the organisations we worked with during the project, and our Vice President **Halil ÖZKARACA** for representing the association.

KOZ-DER will continue to strengthen its youth work from the local to the European level and to create new opportunities for young people.
