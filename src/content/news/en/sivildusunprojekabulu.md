# Our Green Skills Bridge: Waste, Labour and Rights Project Has Been Awarded Support

At KOZ-DER, we are delighted and excited to bring yet another project to life. Our project **“Green Skills Bridge: Waste, Labour and Rights”**, prepared under the Sivil Düşün **“Let’s Close the Gap” Special Support** scheme, has been awarded funding.

## About the Project

The project will be carried out in Kilis, bringing together **young people aged 18 to 30**. Our activities will focus on the environment and sustainability, labour rights, the reuse of waste materials, production, creativity and solidarity.

Through hands-on workshops, we aim to develop participants’ skills in repurposing waste materials. At the same time, we want to strengthen their awareness of fundamental rights in working life, social security mechanisms and the right to a healthy environment.

## What We Aim For

At the end of the project, we will carry out activities that make the experiences and recommendations of the participants visible. We will continue to create together for a greener, fairer and more solidary future.

We are excited about new ideas, new opportunities and the new stories we will create together with young people.
