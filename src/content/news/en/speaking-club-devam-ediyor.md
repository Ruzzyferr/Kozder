# Our Speaking Clubs Keep Going Strong!

🗣️🌍 **Speaking English at its most enjoyable: every week, online and in person!**

**KOZ-DER**’s Speaking Clubs keep growing with new participants every week. On Wednesday evenings we meet online, and on Friday evenings at our centre, to practise speaking English, learn new words and share different perspectives.

## This Week in Kilis: Vampires and Villagers 🧛‍♂️🧑‍🌾

At Friday evening's meeting at our centre we played **Vampires and Villagers**, a social deduction game similar to Mafia! Throughout the game, participants asked questions in English, defended their guesses, tried to persuade each other and reasoned together to find out who the vampire was. With plenty of laughter along the way, it turned out to be one of the most fun ways to use English in natural conversation rather than from memory. 😄

## Online Speaking Club: Wednesday Evenings on Google Meet 💻

Our Online Speaking Club also continues on Wednesday evenings with young people joining from different cities. In this week's session, participants shared their experiences, asked questions and discussed each other's ideas in English.

## Join Us!

Our Speaking Clubs are **open to everyone and free**. Our goal isn't perfect English; it's **finding the courage to speak, practising and learning together.**

- 💻 **Online:** Every **Wednesday at 21:00**, Google Meet — just fill in the level assessment form first: [Online Speaking Club](/en/events/online-speaking-club-2026)
- 📍 **In person:** Every **Friday at 20:00**, **KOZ-DER** centre, Kilis — no registration needed, just come along: [KOZ-DER Speaking Club](/en/events/speakingclub-2026)

To stay up to date, you can also join our Speaking Club **WhatsApp groups**; you can get the group links from the event pages or by writing to us. 📲

**We'd love to see you next week!** 💙
