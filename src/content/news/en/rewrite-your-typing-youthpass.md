# Back from Poland with Youthpass: (RE)WRITE YOUR TYPING Completed

🎓🇵🇱 **Our young people in Poland received their Youthpass certificates!**

The **(RE)WRITE YOUR TYPING** Erasmus+ youth exchange, in which KOZ-DER took part as a sending organisation, took place from 16 to 23 October 2026 in **Puck**, on Poland's Baltic coast. Our young people attended the **Youthpass ceremony** on the last day of the project, received their certificates and successfully completed the process. 👏

## What Did They Do?

The project aimed to replace mindless typing in the digital age with **intentional, creative and genuine communication**. Together with young people from different countries, our participants spent a week:

- creating their own stories in **writing as storytelling** workshops,
- combining words with photography and design in **text and visual art** sessions,
- looking at their surroundings with fresh eyes in the **Color Hunt** outdoor activity,
- discussing the **do's and don'ts of writing in the digital era**,
- reflecting on their own learning journey by writing a **letter to their future self**,
- sharing our culture by introducing Türkiye at the intercultural evening.

## Youthpass: Recognising Learning

**Youthpass** is a European recognition tool that makes the learning gained in Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps activities visible. With it, our young people have documented their development in communication, creativity, teamwork, intercultural awareness and digital skills. It will also be a valuable reference for future education and job opportunities. 🌍
