At our volunteer meet-up in Istanbul we came together with volunteers who have taken part in our projects and with young people interested in international opportunities. In the friendly setting of Espresso Lab Mecidiyeköy we got to know each other and shared our experiences.

## Notes from the Evening

- We talked about European Solidarity Corps (ESC) and Erasmus+ opportunities.
- Volunteers who have taken part in past projects told their own stories.
- We answered questions about new application processes.
- New connections were made between our volunteers.

## Thank You

Thank you to everyone who came and shared their energy with us. See you at the next meet-up.
