**Speaking Club**, which we started so that our volunteers arrive at their projects abroad better prepared, has held its first session. In a relaxed and supportive setting, participants talked in English about everyday topics.

## What Is Speaking Club?

- A pressure-free space for speaking practice, open to all levels
- Designed to build confidence and fluency before a project
- A free volunteer activity that runs regularly

Everyone interested is welcome at the next session. Follow our events and announcements pages for dates.
