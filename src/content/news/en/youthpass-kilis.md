# No More Walls, Europe Calls Project Completed with a Youthpass Ceremony

The Erasmus+ **No More Walls, Europe Calls Youth Exchange Project**, which brought together **25 young people** from Türkiye, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Bulgaria, was completed with a meaningful ceremony where **Youthpass certificates** documenting the participants’ learning outcomes were presented.

Throughout the project, young people from different countries experienced a shared learning process through educational sessions, cultural nights, institutional visits, local activities, city tours and intercultural learning activities. During this process, participants discovered different cultures, learned together, strengthened solidarity and experienced the project’s main message of **overcoming walls through friendship**.

## Youthpass Certificates Were Presented

During the closing programme, **Youthpass certificates**, which make learning outcomes gained in Erasmus+ projects visible, were presented to the participants. The certificate ceremony became a meaningful closing moment where participants reflected on their experiences, skills and intercultural learning processes throughout the project.

Through the Youthpass process, young people had the opportunity to recognise more clearly the experiences they gained in communication, teamwork, intercultural interaction, active participation, responsibility and personal development. The ceremony was not only a closing event, but also a special moment where young people made their own learning journeys visible.

## A Strong Process from Trainings to Cultural Nights

Within the scope of **No More Walls, Europe Calls**, the activities aimed to bring young people together with peers from different cultures, reduce prejudices, meet around common values and build new friendships.

The educational activities focused on inclusion, solidarity, active citizenship, intercultural learning and youth participation. Cultural nights and local activities also contributed to helping participants introduce their own countries, learn about different cultures more closely and create a common space for sharing.
