# This Week at KOZ-DER Speaking Club: Artificial Intelligence

🗣️🤖 **This week, artificial intelligence was on the agenda at KOZ-DER Speaking Club!**

At our Speaking Club, held **every Friday at 20:00** at our centre in Kilis, this week's topic was **"Artificial Intelligence"**.

Starting from a short reading text, participants discussed in English the place of AI in our daily lives, its effects from education to the world of work, the opportunities it offers and the questions it raises. As everyone shared their own experiences and perspectives, the session turned into a lively exchange of ideas. 💡

The aim of Speaking Club is not perfect English; it is **finding the courage to speak, learning new words, listening to different views and expressing yourself more comfortably.** Practising with topics from today's world makes it both useful and fun. 🌍🤝

With KOZ-DER Speaking Club, we keep meeting every week with **a new topic, a new conversation and a new experience**.

📅 **In person:** Every Friday at 20:00, KOZ-DER centre, Kilis — [event page](/en/events/speakingclub-2026)

💻 **Online:** Every Wednesday at 21:00 on Google Meet (starting 14 October) — [Online Speaking Club](/en/events/online-speaking-club-2026)

**See you next week!**
