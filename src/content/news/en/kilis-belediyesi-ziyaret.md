# International Friendship and Cooperation Meeting in Kilis

Within the scope of the **No More Walls, Europe Calls** project, an important meeting was held in Kilis to strengthen international friendship and cooperation.

**Vladislav PETROV**, President of **Life is Beautiful Foundation**, **Emiliya GEORGIEVA**, President of **Children with Special Needs Association**, **Mevlide ÖZKARACA**, President of **KOZ-DER**, **Halil ÖZKARACA**, Project Coordinator, **Mehmet KARAOĞLAN**, faculty member at **Kilis 7 Aralık University**, **Osman ERAY**, President of **Kilis City Council**, and our group leaders from **Azerbaijan, Georgia, Bulgaria and Türkiye** visited Kilis Mayor **Hakan BİLECEN** in his office.

## Thanks Were Expressed for Support and Hospitality

During the meeting, we expressed our gratitude to Kilis Mayor **Hakan BİLECEN** for his support and hospitality during the programme and concert organised the previous day.

The meeting also focused on strengthening the friendship and brotherhood ties between Kilis and **Madan**, Bulgaria, as well as developing new cooperation opportunities between the two cities through youth work, cultural activities and international projects.

## New Cooperation Steps between Kilis and Madan

One of the most meaningful outcomes of the meeting was the invitation extended by the Bulgarian delegation to Kilis Mayor **Hakan BİLECEN** to visit **Madan** in the near future.

We hope that this invitation will become an important starting point for new partnerships between Kilis and Madan, stronger cooperation in the field of youth work and deeper cultural ties between the two cities.

## Friendships Beyond Borders

As KOZ-DER, we would like to thank Kilis Mayor **Hakan BİLECEN** for his kind reception, his support for youth work and his positive approach to international cooperation.

We believe that friendships beyond borders bring cities and young people closer together, and we hope that this cooperation process extending from Kilis to Madan will turn into stronger partnerships in the future.
