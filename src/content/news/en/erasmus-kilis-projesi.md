# Rectorate Visit Held within the Scope of the No More Walls, Europe Calls Project

Within the scope of the Erasmus+ project **No More Walls, Europe Calls**, implemented by **Children with Special Needs**, an important institutional visit was carried out during the project’s hosting process in Kilis.

As part of the project activities, **Kilis 7 Aralık University** played an important role in hosting the participants and supporting the implementation process in Kilis. In this context, KOZ-DER representatives and project stakeholders visited the **Rectorate of Kilis 7 Aralık University**.

## Cooperation and Future Projects Were Discussed

During the meeting, the current cooperation between **KOZ-DER**, **Kilis 7 Aralık University** and **Children with Special Needs** was discussed. The meeting also focused on possible future collaborations in the fields of youth work, Erasmus+ projects, European Solidarity Corps activities and international partnerships.

It was emphasized that cooperation between institutions should not be limited only to the current project, but should be strengthened through new joint activities that support the social, cultural, academic and international development of young people.

## Thanks Were Expressed for Hosting

KOZ-DER and project stakeholders expressed their gratitude to **Kilis 7 Aralık University** for its contribution and hosting support during the implementation of the No More Walls, Europe Calls project in Kilis.

The university’s open and supportive approach to international youth projects made an important contribution to helping young people from different countries get to know Kilis and experience the university environment.

As KOZ-DER, we continue to support activities that bring young people from different cultures together, create shared learning spaces and encourage young people to become more active at the European level.

## From Local Cooperation to International Partnership

This visit was an important step in combining KOZ-DER’s strong local institutional relations with international partnerships. The discussions held between **Kilis 7 Aralık University**, **Children with Special Needs** and **KOZ-DER** are expected to contribute to more inclusive, sustainable and youth-focused projects in the future.

As KOZ-DER, we would like to thank **Kilis 7 Aralık University**, **Children with Special Needs** and all stakeholders who contributed to this cooperation process.
