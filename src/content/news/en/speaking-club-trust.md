# This Week at KOZ-DER Speaking Club: Trust

🗣️🤝 **This week at Speaking Club, we talked about trust!**

At our Speaking Club, held **every Friday at 20:00** at our centre in Kilis, this week's topic was **"Trust"**.

Starting from a reading text, participants discussed in English how trust is built in friendships, families, working life and the digital world, why it can be shaken so easily and whether it can be rebuilt. As everyone shared their own experiences and perspectives, the conversation became a warm exchange of different ideas. 💬

## A Fun Finale with Charades 🎬

After the discussion, participants played **charades**. Trying to guess the names on the cards held up to their foreheads, we laughed a lot and practised asking questions, describing and giving clues in English along the way. One of this week's cards was **Beethoven**! 🎹😄

The aim of Speaking Club is not perfect English; it is **finding the courage to speak, learning new words, listening to different views and having fun while doing it.** 🌍
