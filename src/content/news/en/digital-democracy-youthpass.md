# Back from Pazardzhik with Youthpass: Digital Democracy Youth Exchange Completed

🇧🇬🎓 **A week for young people's voices in the digital world: our team received their Youthpass certificates!**

The **Digital Democracy: Youth Voices Online!** Erasmus+ youth exchange, in which **KOZ-DER** took part as a partner organisation, took place from 28 October to 6 November 2026 in **Pazardzhik**, Bulgaria. Hosted by **FORUM Youth Activities Center**, the project brought together young people from Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, Portugal, Slovakia and Türkiye.

The **KOZ-DER** team was made up of **6 people**. Three of our group members successfully completed the visa process to be able to join the project, so after a demanding preparation period our team set off at full strength. ✈️

## What Did They Do?

Over a week of non-formal learning, our participants:

- discussed ways to make their voices heard online in **digital democracy and youth participation** workshops,
- tackled disinformation, hate speech and digital discrimination through **media literacy and critical thinking** activities,
- developed ideas on transparency, equality and democratic participation in **active citizenship** sessions,
- created together with young people from other countries through **simulations, role plays and group work**,
- designed their own awareness campaigns in the **digital campaign lab**,
- introduced Türkiye and got to know other cultures at the **intercultural evenings**.

## They Presented KOZ-DER 🤝

In the organisation presentation session, our young people introduced **KOZ-DER** to the international participants. They talked about our mission, our mind games, coding, Speaking Club and book club activities in Kilis, Erasmus+ and European Solidarity Corps (ESC) opportunities, and how young people can take part in them. The presentation also opened the door to conversations about new cooperation and partnerships.

## Learning Recognised with Youthpass 🎓

The programme ended with participants receiving their **Youthpass certificates**. Youthpass is a Europe-wide tool that recognises the competences young people gain through non-formal learning. With this project, our young people documented their experience particularly in **digital literacy, media awareness, critical thinking, communication, teamwork and intercultural awareness**.

## Thank You

We warmly thank **FORUM Youth Activities Center** for hosting this meaningful experience, as well as the trainers, all partner organisations and our young people who represented us so well. 💙
